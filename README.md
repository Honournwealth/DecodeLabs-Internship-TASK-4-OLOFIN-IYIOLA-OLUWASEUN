# 📊 Sales Performance & Customer Insight Dashboard

## 🚀 Project Overview
This project is a comprehensive **Power BI Dashboard Solution** developed during the final phase of my online internship at **Decolabs**.  

The project transforms raw e-commerce transactional data into meaningful business insights using interactive dashboards, KPI tracking, and data storytelling techniques.

The solution focuses on:
- Sales performance monitoring  
- Customer purchasing behavior  
- Product performance analysis  
- Marketing effectiveness  
- Operational efficiency tracking  

The dashboards were designed to help business stakeholders make informed, data-driven decisions.

---

# 📌 Dashboard Pages

# 1️⃣ Sales Performance Dashboard

The Sales Performance Dashboard provides a high-level overview of revenue generation, customer activity, and order trends across the business.

## 🔑 Key KPIs
- **Total Revenue:** 942.4K  
- **Total Orders:** 891  
- **Total Customers:** 886  
- **Average Order Value (AOV):** 1.06K  
- **Cancellation Rate:** 22%  

---

## 📈 Performance Analysis

### ✅ What Is Performing Well
- **Printers generated the highest revenue** among all product categories, making them the strongest-performing product line.
- **Instagram and Email referral sources** produced the highest number of customer orders, showing strong digital marketing performance.
- Revenue remained relatively stable between **2023 and 2025**, with multiple sales peaks above **6K**, indicating consistent business activity.
- The business maintained a strong customer-to-order ratio, showing repeat and active customer engagement.

### ❌ What Is Not Performing Well
- The **22% cancellation rate** is relatively high and may negatively affect profitability and customer satisfaction.
- Products such as **Phones and Desks** generated comparatively lower revenue.
- Referral sources such as **Facebook and Referral traffic** contributed fewer orders compared to Instagram and Email campaigns.

---

## 💡 Recommendations
- Increase marketing investment in **Instagram and Email campaigns** since they generate the strongest order performance.
- Investigate causes of cancellations such as delayed delivery, payment issues, or stock availability.
- Introduce promotional campaigns or discounts for lower-performing products like Phones and Desks.
- Optimize referral programs to improve underperforming traffic sources.

# 2️⃣ Product & Customer Insight Analysis

This dashboard focuses on customer behavior, purchasing habits, and product sales trends.

## 🔑 Key KPIs
- **Total Revenue:** 942.4K  
- **Total Quantity Sold:** 3K  
- **Average Items Per Cart:** 5.46  
- **Coupon Usage Rate:** 100%  
- **Average Customer Spend:** 1.06K  

---

## 📈 Performance Analysis

### ✅ What Is Performing Well
- Customers purchased an average of **5 items per cart**, indicating strong basket sizes and good upselling opportunities.
- Revenue and quantity sold followed similar monthly patterns, suggesting consistent demand behavior.
- Peak business performance occurred around **June**, where both revenue and quantity sold reached their highest levels.
- Payment methods showed balanced usage distribution, reducing dependency on a single payment channel.
- Most customers purchased between **4 and 7 items per order**, indicating healthy customer purchasing activity.

### ❌ What Is Not Performing Well
- Revenue and quantity sold dropped significantly around **July**, indicating a possible seasonal decline or reduced customer engagement.
- Lower-performing products such as **Phones and Desks** consistently contributed less revenue.
- A **100% coupon usage rate** may indicate excessive discount dependency, which could reduce profit margins.

---

## 💡 Recommendations
- Analyze the cause of the July sales decline and introduce seasonal campaigns during low-performing periods.
- Reduce excessive coupon dependency by introducing loyalty rewards or targeted discounts instead of broad promotions.
- Bundle lower-performing products with high-performing products like Printers and Tablets.
- Use customer segmentation strategies to improve targeted marketing and personalized promotions.

# 3️⃣ Order Operations & Marketing Dashboard

This dashboard tracks operational efficiency and evaluates marketing channel effectiveness.

## 🔑 Key KPIs
- **Delivered Orders:** 179  
- **Cancelled Orders:** 192  
- **Returned Orders:** 171  
- **Total Tracking Orders:** 891  
- **Cancellation Rate:** 22%  

---

## 📈 Performance Analysis

### ✅ What Is Performing Well
- **Instagram generated the highest revenue contribution**, making it the most effective marketing channel.
- Payment methods maintained a balanced distribution, suggesting flexibility and accessibility for customers.
- Order tracking capabilities improved visibility into operational activities.

### ❌ What Is Not Performing Well
- Cancelled orders represented the largest order category at approximately **21.55%**, indicating operational inefficiencies.
- Returned orders were also significantly high at **171**, which may suggest customer dissatisfaction, product quality concerns, or delivery issues.
- Cancellation spikes increased between **2024 and 2025**, showing worsening operational performance over time.

---

## 💡 Recommendations
- Improve delivery timelines and customer communication to reduce cancellations and returns.
- Conduct root-cause analysis on returned products to identify quality or fulfillment issues.
- Strengthen inventory management to reduce stock-related cancellations.
- Expand investment in high-performing channels like Instagram while improving weak-performing channels such as Facebook referrals.
